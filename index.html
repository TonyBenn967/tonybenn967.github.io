Common questions

I bought “Remove ads” but I still see ads. Open the ☰ menu and tap “Restore purchase”, using the same Apple Account you bought it with. Optional “watch an ad for new pieces” videos stay available because you choose to watch them.

Will I lose my progress? Your current game and best score are saved on your phone. Deleting the app removes them.

Does it work offline? Yes. The game works without internet; ads only appear when you’re connected.
