Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Tidy Blocks app (“the app”) uses and why. The app is made by [Tony Benn], an individual developer based in the United Kingdom (“I”, “me”). I am responsible for your information under UK data protection law (UK GDPR). You can contact me at [tonybenn967@proton.me].

The short version

  • There are no accounts. I don’t ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. I never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your current game, best score, number of games played, sound setting and whether you’ve bought “Remove ads”. This stays on your device, is never sent to me, and is deleted if you delete the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. To do this, Google may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, device type and operating system, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud.

When you first open the app you may be asked for consent to personalised ads, and Apple will ask whether you allow the app to track you. If you say no, you’ll still see ads, but they will be less personalised. You can change these choices at any time in your iPhone’s Settings (Privacy & Security → Tracking). You can learn more about how Google uses information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

The legal basis for personalised advertising is your consent. For non-personalised ads and fraud prevention, it is my legitimate interest in funding a free app.

Purchases

If you buy “Remove ads”, the payment is processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. I only receive confirmation that the purchase happened, not your name or payment details.

Children

The app is a simple puzzle game suitable for all ages, but it is not specifically directed at children. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child’s information has been collected, contact me and I will help.

Sharing your information

I don’t sell your information. The only third parties involved are Google (for ads) and Apple (for downloads and purchases), as described above. They may process information outside the UK under their own safeguards.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because I don’t hold information that identifies you, most requests are best handled through your device settings or directly with Google. You can still contact me with any question. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
